## Problem

Evaluating an AI system involves more than checking how accurately it performs. Issues like uneven data distributions and missing documentation can be overlooked, especially when the information is scattered across different files and tools. We wanted to bring those checks together and make the findings easier to interpret.

## What it does

The toolkit analyzes datasets for potential imbalances, checks for missing model documentation, and presents the findings through charts and summaries. Users can review the results in one interface and generate downloadable PDF reports highlighting potential risks and areas that need attention.
