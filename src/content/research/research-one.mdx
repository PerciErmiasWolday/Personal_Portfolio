## Abstract

I'm working with Professor Jack Bandy at UIC to study how consistently large language models make ethical judgments. We're comparing responses from Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok to see whether they apply similar ethical principles and how their conclusions change when questions are framed differently. I use SQL to analyze patterns of agreement, disagreement, and inconsistency across the models.

## Research question

**Do AI models apply the same ethical standards when the underlying situation stays the same?**

We're interested in both consistency within individual models and differences between models. A key challenge is determining whether a change in judgment reflects a meaningful difference in context or simply how the question was worded.

## Method

I use SQL to organize and compare model responses across ethical scenarios and prompt variations. The analysis examines differences in conclusions, supporting reasoning, and sensitivity to framing, allowing us to identify patterns beyond individual responses.

## Findings

**Research in progress.** We're currently analyzing cross-model and within-model consistency. Final findings are not yet available.

## Why it matters

An AI response can sound reasonable without being consistent. As people increasingly rely on these systems for advice and decision-making, understanding whether they apply stable ethical principles becomes essential.

Our research aims to contribute to more rigorous ways of evaluating AI reliability, fairness, and accountability.
