I started college at Alma College in Michigan before eventually finding my way to Chicago. Somewhere along the way, I've gotten involved in a little bit of everything, from starting a Girls Who Code chapter to teaching Global Affairs in India, working at a startup, and building projects of my own.

I think I've always been someone who likes to figure things out as I go. At Alma, I started Girls Who Code because I wanted more women to have a space where they could explore programming and learn from each other. And sometimes the motivation is much closer to home, like building ReadingBridge after seeing my autistic cousin struggle with reading comprehension.

Looking back, some of my favorite experiences started with noticing something I wished were different and deciding to give it a try. I don't always know exactly how to do something when I start, but I enjoy learning, working with different people, and seeing what I can make happen.
